How We Work

Our Professional Cleaning Process

Every job begins with a personal inspection and ends with customer satisfaction. We never rush through your home; instead, we take the time to understand your specific cleaning needs and fabric types.

Step-by-Step Carpet and Upholstery Cleaning Process

1

Pre-Inspection

We walk through each room, identifying stains, traffic areas, and problem spots.

2

Pre-Treatment

Specialized solutions are applied to loosen soil and oils from carpet fibers or upholstery fabric.

3

Deep Extraction

Using our truck-mounted system, we remove dirt, allergens, and moisture, leaving surfaces dry in 4-6 hours.

4

Spot Treatment and Grooming

We target stubborn stains and carefully groom fibers for a smooth, even finish.

5

Post-Inspection

Before we leave, we review every cleaned area with you to ensure complete satisfaction.

From start to finish, our process is thorough, efficient, and focused on long-term results.

Our Services

Comprehensive Cleaning Services

Your carpets, rugs, and furniture see daily wear and tear, spills, dirt, pet accidents, and more. That's why we offer a full range of cleaning and restoration services tailored to each surface.

Our Specialized Cleaning Solutions Include:

Carpet Cleaning

Deep hot-water extraction to remove embedded dirt, bacteria, and allergens, restoring the look and feel of your carpet.

Rug Cleaning

Safe handling for delicate area rugs, including wool, Persian, and synthetic materials. If needed, we clean both sides to ensure complete soil removal.

Carpet Repair

Stretching, patching, and reseaming damaged carpet sections to eliminate ripples or tears.

Upholstery Cleaning

Gentle cleaning for sofas, chairs, and fabric headboards, removing stains, oils, and odors while protecting color and texture.

Pet Odor & Stain Treatment

Enzyme and encapsulants that neutralize odors at the source, not just mask them.
