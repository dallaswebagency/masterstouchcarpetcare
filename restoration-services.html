How We Work

Our Comprehensive Restoration Process

Restoration begins with understanding the problem. We don't just treat the surface, we address the root cause to prevent future issues and ensure long-term protection for your home or business.

Step-by-Step Water Damage Restoration Process

1

Inspection and Assessment

We evaluate the affected areas to determine the type of water damage, clean, gray, or black water, and the safest cleaning approach.

2

Water Extraction

Using professional-grade equipment, we remove standing water quickly to prevent further damage and mold growth.

3

Drying and Dehumidifying

We use air movers and dehumidifiers to eliminate moisture from carpets, padding, and subfloors.

4

Cleaning and Sanitizing

We clean all surfaces using safe antimicrobial treatments to prevent bacteria and odor buildup.

5

Restoration

We use specialized tools to ensure walls and floors are completely dry.
