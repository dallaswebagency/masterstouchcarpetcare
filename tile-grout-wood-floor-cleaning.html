How We Work

Our Tile, Grout, and Wood Floor Cleaning Process

Every service begins with an inspection and ends with a gleaming finish. We tailor each cleaning process to the specific flooring type, whether it's ceramic tile, natural stone, or genuine hardwood.

Inspection

Pre-Treatment

After applying a professional grade solution, we use a scrubber that loosens dirt, grease, and residue embedded in grout pores.

4

Neutralizing Rinse

We balance pH levels to leave your tile clean and residue-free.

5

Optional Grout Sealing

Adds a protective barrier to resist stains, moisture, and mold.
